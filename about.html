The Beginning: A Story of Quality and Reliability.

Wabane Chemicals is a South African manufacturer of cleaning chemicals and supplier of consumables that go with it, serving businesses and households.

Our story

Wabane Chemicals is a 100% black owned and South African company which primarily manufactures and distributes household and industrial cleaning as well as specialized chemicals. The company was established in 2012 by TC Motsepe and XMC Sithebe having bachelor’s degrees in chemistry and Biochemistry respectively plus a decade of experience.

Through natural progression, the secondary part of the business is the supply of cleaning materials and engineering tools. Company manufactures and distributes ground-breaking chemical advancement formulated to outperform on application.

The Bigger Picture

Customer Service

An excellent customer service is at the core of our business. We have a 24hr order lead time for orders placed before 11am. If this cannot be achieved, the customer will be communicated to for them to plan accordingly. As far as possible we will assist our customers with same day deliveries. We’re more than happy to accommodate our customers favourable terms (i.e. COD, 30-Days and in certain instances 60-Days).
