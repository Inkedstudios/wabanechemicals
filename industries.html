Products built for how hard you work.

Every industry has different surfaces, soils and rules. We will help you choose the products that fit your needs.

Hospitality

Hotels, lodges and restaurants need spotless rooms, kitchens and laundries every day.

We supply: Multi-surface cleaners, bathroom care, laundry liquids, floor cleaners, sanitisers, cloths and bin liners.

Food and Beverages

Processing plants and commercial kitchens need strict hygiene and food-safe products.

We supply: Food-safe degreasers, surface cleaners and hand hygiene.

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics and care facilities require stringent cleanliness and infection control.

We supply: Industrial-strength floor cleaners, degreasers and sanitizers.

Manufacturing, Mining and Engineering

Workshops and production lines build up oil, grease and industrial soils.

We supply: Heavy-duty degreasers, parts and floor cleaners, hand cleaners and rags.

Automotive and Logistics

Fleets, workshops and car washes need fast, tough cleaning across large volumes.

We supply: Truck and vehicle wash concentrates, degreasers, floor cleaners and microfibre cloths.

Education and Public Facilities

Schools, colleges and municipal buildings need safe, economical cleaning at scale.

We supply: Neutral floor cleaners, toilet and bathroom cleaners, paper products and bulk consumables.

Cleaning Contractors

Professional cleaners need products that perform and pack sizes that make sense.

We supply: Full chemical range in concentrates, plus mops, cloths, gloves and bags on one invoice.

Retail and Wholesale

Shops and distributors need reliable stock, good margins and appealing packs.

We supply: Domestic range in retail sizes, private-label options and mixed pallets.
